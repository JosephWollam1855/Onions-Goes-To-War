How to Play War

  1. Shuffle one 52-card deck without Jokers. Deal 26 cards face down to each player.
  2. Press Flip Cards. Both players reveal the card on top of their piles. Ace is highest, followed by King down to 2.
  3. The player with the higher card collects both cards and places them at the bottom of their deck.
  4. If the ranks tie, it's WAR! Each player places up to three cards face down, then flips one face up. The higher card wins every card in the middle. Repeated ties mean another War.
  5. If there aren't enough cards for a full War, a player places as many as possible and uses the final card face up. If one player has no cards left to reveal, the other player wins the pot. If both run out on the same tie, the pot is awarded randomly to end the deadlock.
  6. Win by collecting all 52 cards. Some games can last a long time.

Both player modes use the same rules. In local play, each person takes a side of the table; one button flips both cards together. Auto Play flips automatically at a moderate pace.
